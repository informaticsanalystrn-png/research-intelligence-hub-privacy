1. About this policy

Research Intelligence Hub ("the Service") is a research and knowledge-management project. Its planned Pinterest integration is designed to let the account owner authorize the Service to read information from that owner's Pinterest account through Pinterest's official API.

This policy describes the information the Service is designed to handle, why it is used, how it is protected, and the choices available to the account owner. The Pinterest integration is under development. Features that are not yet deployed are described as planned practices, not as currently operating features. This policy must be reviewed and updated before any material change in data handling.

The Service is independently developed and is not operated, sponsored, or endorsed by Pinterest, Inc.

2. Information the Service may access

If and when the account owner connects Pinterest through Pinterest's OAuth authorization process, and only to the extent allowed by the permissions approved by Pinterest, the Service may access:

  • Basic Pinterest account information made available through the authorized API, such as an account identifier and display name.
  • Board information, such as board identifiers, names, descriptions, and visibility information returned by the API.
  • Pin information, such as Pin identifiers, descriptions, source or destination links, board associations, creation or update timestamps, and media metadata returned by the API.
  • OAuth authorization credentials, including access tokens and refresh tokens, needed to maintain the authorized connection.

The Service does not ask for or collect a Pinterest password, session cookie, or one-time login code. Authentication is intended to occur through Pinterest's official authorization flow.

The Service does not intentionally request information from Pinterest accounts that the account owner has not authorized or content that the account owner is not permitted to access.

3. How information is used

Information obtained through the Pinterest API is intended to be used only to provide the account owner's research and organization functions, including:

  • Listing and organizing the account owner's authorized boards and Pins.
  • Maintaining source links and available attribution information.
  • Categorizing records, identifying duplicates, and supporting search.
  • Creating research indexes and records for the account owner's own use.
  • Troubleshooting, maintaining, and securing the authorized integration.

The Service will not use Pinterest API information to build advertising audiences, conduct ad targeting, sell data, or create a data-broker product.

4. AI and automated analysis

The project may include AI-assisted research features. Pinterest API information will not be submitted to an external AI provider or other third-party analysis service unless that use has first been reviewed and is permitted by Pinterest's applicable terms and policies, and any required disclosures and permissions have been addressed.

Until that review is completed, Pinterest-derived information must be processed only in an approved environment controlled by the Service operator. The Service will not use Pinterest API information to train a general-purpose AI model.

5. Sharing and disclosure

The Service does not sell Pinterest API information or disclose it to advertisers, data brokers, unrelated applications, or other third parties.

Pinterest-derived information may be made available to the account owner whose Pinterest account supplied that information. It will not be made publicly available through a public repository, public dataset, public demonstration, or public-facing research library.

Any future use of hosting, storage, analytics, or other service providers that could receive Pinterest API information must be reviewed for consistency with Pinterest's developer requirements and applicable law before that use begins. This policy will be updated if the Service's data-sharing practices materially change.

The Service may disclose information if required by law or valid legal process, or when reasonably necessary to protect the rights, security, or integrity of the Service and its users.

6. Storage and security

The Service is being designed to:

  • Keep application secrets and OAuth tokens out of source code, public files, and ordinary logs.
  • Restrict access to credentials and Pinterest-derived records to the Service operator.
  • Use access controls and secure storage appropriate to the type of information.
  • Avoid placing Pinterest-derived records in public repositories, public workflow logs, or public downloadable artifacts.
  • Validate authorization requests and protect the OAuth callback against cross-site request forgery by validating the OAuth state value.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. The Service operator will take reasonable measures appropriate to the project but cannot guarantee absolute security.

The integration is still in development. These statements describe the intended security design; they should not be read as confirmation that every control has already been implemented.

7. Retention and deletion

Information will be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the research functions described in this policy, maintain the authorized connection, meet applicable legal obligations, resolve disputes, and protect the Service.

The account owner may disconnect the Pinterest integration and request deletion of Pinterest-derived information by contacting the privacy contact listed at the top of this policy. The request should identify the Pinterest account or connection involved without including passwords, access tokens, or other secrets.

After a verified deletion request, the Service operator will delete the relevant Pinterest-derived records and revoke or delete stored authorization credentials under the operator's control, subject to any legal retention requirement. Residual copies in system backups, if any, will be deleted or overwritten according to the applicable backup cycle.

A specific retention schedule and backup-deletion period must be documented before the integration is opened to additional users.

8. Disconnecting Pinterest authorization

The account owner may revoke the Service's access through the account and application authorization controls provided by Pinterest. Revoking access prevents future API requests that depend on that authorization. The account owner may also contact the privacy contact above to request deletion of stored information.

Revoking authorization at Pinterest does not automatically guarantee that copies previously stored by the Service have been deleted; a separate deletion request may be needed.

9. Third-party services and external links

The Service may link to Pinterest and to websites identified in Pin destination URLs. Those websites and Pinterest are operated by others and have their own privacy policies and practices. The Service is not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites.

The Service will identify and review any infrastructure or service provider that may process Pinterest-derived information before enabling that processing.

10. Children's privacy

The Service is not designed for children under 13 and is not intended to collect personal information from children under 13.

11. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the Service develops or as legal, security, or platform requirements change. The "Last updated" date will be revised when changes are made. Material changes to the Service's use of Pinterest information will be reviewed before implementation and, where required, submitted to Pinterest for review.
